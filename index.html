Ossuary

A first-person automation game inspired by Sedlec Ossuary, where you turn recovered bones into elaborate monuments.

Excavate burial sites, sort materials and recruit workers to automate your growing operation across a persistent campaign.

Black Tide:Forsaken

A historical open-world action RPG set on the real island of Lindisfarne during the Viking Age.

Inspired by Kingdom Come: Deliverance, it will retell selected events from the Viking Age in Britain through a historically accurate story. Lindisfarne is faithfully recreated using real-world geographic height data, with hand-built landmarks including Lindisfarne Monastery.

↓

Drone
Defence

A VR survival game where you fight increasingly dangerous drones in a military base.

Aim with both hands, reload manually and carry equipment on your body, with upgrades and a run-based economy across campaign and endless modes.

King’s
Table

A 1v1 digital adaptation of Copenhagen Hnefatafl, where attackers pursue a king protected by his defenders.

The planned experience places the board in a 3D tavern, with casual Steam play and ranked matches.
