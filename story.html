Our story.

ZeYDi Games is an independent solo dev studio making games spanning 3D action RPG adventures, VR survival experiences, incremental factory builders grounded in historical story, 3D multiplayer party games, and digitised ranked historical board games.

Our purpose

I make games to try and create truly memorable gaming experiences: a plan starts working, a difficult action becomes familiar, enjoying a party game with your friends late into the evening, learning about English history whilst being immersed in a virtual world and so much more!

My purpose is to create experiences that truly inspire a feeling of exploration wanting to play and discover more. Similar to the feelings I get when playing my favourite games such as Red Dead Redemption 2, Kingdom Come: Deliverance, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, RuneScape, Rust, DayZ, The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim and so many more!

The games

My games so far span multiple genres but all have the same purpose of celebrating history and culture, or giving friends a platform to have great experiences together. Explore games such as rebuilding a real-world place in Sedlec Ossuary in Ossuary, going on an epic action RPG adventure around a faithfully recreated Viking Age Lindisfarne in Black Tide: Forsaken, or the VR multiplayer survival experience of Drone Defence.

Enjoy a hilarious party game in So You Think You Can Talk?, built around conversation and quick thinking.

I’m bringing Hnefatafl to a 3D tavern in King’s Table, using the modern Copenhagen rules. The game belongs to the wider tafl family, with traditions across Norse Scandinavia, Anglo-Saxon England, Ireland and Wales. Related games include Irish brandubh, Welsh tawlbwrdd and Sámi tablut, recorded by Carl Linnaeus in 1732. It is a chance to celebrate centuries of strategy and give players a new way to share it.

Our approach

I want each game to have a clear identity. The setting, the controls and the systems should support the same experience, whether that is learning a process, exploring a place or playing with other people.

As development continues, my focus is on making those central interactions clear, satisfying and worth returning to.
