Our story.

ZeYDi Games is an independent studio making games around exploration, hands-on play and shared experiences.

Our purpose

We make games for the moments when something clicks: a plan starts working, a difficult action becomes familiar, or someone finally guesses the word you have been trying to describe.

Our purpose is to create experiences that invite you to pay attention, try an idea and discover what happens next.

The games behind it

That can take the form of a growing operation in Ossuary, exploring Viking Age Lindisfarne in Black Tide: Forsaken, or the physical decisions of surviving in Drone Defence.

It can also start at a table. So You Think You Can Talk? is built around conversation and quick thinking. King’s Table explores a different kind of exchange: the decisions between two opponents.

Our approach

We want each game to have a clear identity. The setting, the controls and the systems should support the same experience, whether that is learning a process, exploring a place or playing with other people.

As development continues, our focus is on making those central interactions clear, satisfying and worth returning to.
